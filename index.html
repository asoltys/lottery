how it works · non-custodial
Your stake isn't an IOU we hold for you. The moment you enter, your contribution is recorded on the contract as a Shadowing claim tied to your key — so the jackpot is always exactly the sum of every player's claims, never an opaque balance the operator controls.
The engine renders that pot as a timeout tree of per-player VTXOs: each claim is a Bitcoin output you can sweep with only your key after a short timelock — no operator signature needed. Hit your exit proof (it appears above once you've staked) to see the exact VTXO and the CSV exit script that pays it to you.
The draw is provably fair: the winner is picked from a Bitcoin block hash no one can predict or grind. Click any round number under "recent draws" to recompute and verify it yourself.
the cryptography, briefly
Claims use Cube's OP_SHADOW_* opcodes (invariant: contract balance ≥ Σ claims, enforced by the VM). Each VTXO's key path is a Projector value-bound MuSig2 aggregate of your key + the engine key, so a claim can't be re-bound to a different amount; the script path is your unilateral <delay> CSV DROP <your key> CHECKSIG exit. Across rounds the pot's covenant is refreshed by an N-of-N signature of all participants; anyone who goes offline is covered by a pre-signed unroll that puts their VTXO on-chain so they can still exit alone. Today the engine derives and serves each player's exit proof from live claims; emitting those covenants on-chain every round (so the timelocked exit is enforced without trusting the engine to stay honest) is the final piece being rolled out.
Running on Mutinynet (a Bitcoin signet with 30-second blocks).
